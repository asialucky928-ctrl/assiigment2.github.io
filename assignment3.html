History

Pakistan became an independent country on 14 August 1947. Its history includes ancient civilizations and many important cultural periods.

Geography

Pakistan has high mountains, fertile plains, deserts, rivers and a coastline along the Arabian Sea.

People

Pakistan has people from many different regions. Each region has its own traditions, clothing, music and celebrations.

Languages

Urdu and English are widely used in Pakistan. Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto and Balochi are also important regional languages.
